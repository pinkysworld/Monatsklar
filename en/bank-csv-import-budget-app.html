Import bank transactions from CSV into your budget

Monatsklar does not connect to your bank. You export transactions as a CSV file and read them in locally, with no online banking credentials.

How the import works

  1. In your bank’s online banking in a browser, export your transactions as CSV (choose the period, use the export or download function).
  2. In Monatsklar open “Bank import” (Ctrl+8) and drag the file in.
  3. If the format is recognised, Monatsklar says so. Otherwise you map date and amount (optionally payee and description) once and save an import profile.
  4. Pick the target account, check the preview, import. Entries that already exist are marked “already present”.

Recognition applies to specific export variants, not to every product a bank offers. Banks change their exports from time to time; “verified” is no guarantee for every account. German bank formats are supported as well – see the German page.

General export tips

Where the export function sits differs by bank and changes over time. It is usually in the transaction list of your account in browser-based online banking rather than the phone app. Search your bank’s help for “export transactions” or “CSV”. Other banks and spreadsheet files often work through manual column mapping but may need some preparation.

Common pitfalls

  • Signs: some exports show card spending as positive amounts. Check the preview; signs can be inverted during import.
  • Decimal separator and dates: comma or point, DD/MM or MM/DD – adjustable in the advanced mapping. Slash dates are ambiguous, so check them before importing.
  • Encoding: UTF-8, UTF-16 with a byte-order mark and, as a fallback, Windows-1252 are read. Check special characters in the preview.
  • Currency: one book has one currency and nothing is converted. Split multi-currency files first.
  • Re-imports: entries that already exist are marked; you can untick single rows.

When you assign imported rows to a category, Monatsklar remembers it for that payee and assigns it automatically next time.
