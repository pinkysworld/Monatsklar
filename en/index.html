Frequently asked questions

Read the full guide ↗
Do I need internet or an account?

The app works offline without an app account. Buying and installing through the Microsoft Store needs internet access and may require a Microsoft account.

Does Monatsklar connect to my bank?

No. Export transactions from your bank as CSV, then import the file into Monatsklar. Your bank login is never needed.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Export entries as CSV, print reports or save them as PDF, and back up your book file. CSV exports are not encrypted.

How do currencies work?

Choose one currency per book. All accounts and entries in that book use the same currency. There is no automatic currency conversion.
